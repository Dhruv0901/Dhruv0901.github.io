# Dhruv Mehan — Business Systems, Data & Responsible AI

I work from the roots of a business problem: the people doing the work, the decisions they make, the data they trust, and the workarounds that keep operations moving. From there, I build useful, inspectable systems across business analysis, process improvement, data, MLOps, and responsible AI.

**Interactive portfolio:** [dhruv0901.github.io](https://dhruv0901.github.io/)

## Selected projects

- [Future Sales Forecast](https://github.com/Dhruv0901/Future-Sales-Forecast) — an end-to-end retail forecasting workflow using 1.2M transactions, pricing, inventory, promotions, and calendar-aware features.
- [Invoice Generation Service](https://github.com/Dhruv0901/Invoice-Generation-Service) — an auditable MCP-style service connecting demand forecasting, a local language model, and document generation.
- [Coles-AIOps](https://github.com/Dhruv0901/Coles-AIOps) — an OpenCV operational prototype exploring hand-movement tracking, noise reduction, and privacy-preserving face blur.
- [Skin Cancer Classifier](https://github.com/Dhruv0901/Skin-Cancer-Classifier-) — a modular ResNet18 research pipeline with training, evaluation, inference, and Grad-CAM explainability.
- [Mobility & COVID-19](https://github.com/Dhruv0901/Mobility-Trends-to-Decipher-Covid-19-Cases) — a Power BI and Python study connecting Australian mobility trends with public-health data.
- [Australian Inflation Crisis](https://github.com/Dhruv0901/Australian-Inflation-Crisis) — a business analysis briefing translating economic drivers into decision-ready implications.

## What I care about

- Understanding operational reality before proposing technology
- Connecting stakeholder needs, process design, and measurable evidence
- Building systems that remain explainable after the demo
- Making limitations visible rather than hiding them
